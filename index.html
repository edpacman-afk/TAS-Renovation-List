Renovation List

TAS Renovation List is the fast and simple way to document project scopes, site walks, and field notes directly from your phone or tablet. Designed for contractors, project managers, estimators, inspectors and field teams. The app helps you keep all project details organized in one place.

Create detailed scope notes in seconds, organize projects by property or client, and quickly export your information when it's time to share with your team or customers.

Key Features:

- Create and organize unlimited project scope notes

- Store all project information locally on your device

- Fast and simple note-taking interface designed for field use

- Export notes as PDF or TXT files

- Backup projects with Google Drive integration

- Organize notes by project, unit, or property

- Access your project information anytime, even offline

- Clean, easy-to-use interface optimized for productivity

Perfect For:

- Construction projects

- Property management

- Renovation scopes

- Site inspections

- Maintenance documentation

- Estimating and bidding

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 14, 2026

Thank you for using TAS Renovation List. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, Information We Collect. The App is designed to minimize data collection.

Information Stored Locally: Project information, notes, photos, and related content created within the App are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or back up your data.

Automatically Collected Information: The App may collect limited technical information such as crash reports and basic diagnostic data provided by Apple to help improve app stability and performance.

How We Use Information: Any information collected is used only to provide and maintain app functionality, enable file export, and backup features, improve app performance and reliability, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Data Storage and Security: Your project data is primarily stored on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third-Party Services: The app may use third-party services (Apple App Store services and Google Drive API services), these services have their own privacy policies and terms..

Children's Privacy: The app is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices: You may delete your data direcly from the App, and stop using the App at any time.

Changes to This Privacy Policy: We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updats will be reflected by revising the effective date above.
