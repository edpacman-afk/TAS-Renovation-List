Privacy Policy

Thank you for using ProjectScope (TAS Renovation List). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect.

The App is designed to minimize data collection.

Information Stored Locally.

Project information, notes, photos, and related content created within the App are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or back up your data.

Automatically Collected Information.

The App may collect limited technical information such as crash reports and basic diagnostic data provided by Apple to help improve app stability and performance.

How We Use Information.

Any information collected is used only to:.

• Provide and maintain app functionality.

• Enable file export and backup features.

• Improve app performance and reliability.

• Troubleshoot technical issues.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your project data is primarily stored on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including:

• Apple App Store services

• Google Drive API services

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You may:

• Delete your data directly from the App

• Remove backup files from your Google Drive account

• Stop using the App at any time

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the effective date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
